Ordinary
Smordinary

Different is a beautiful thing.

What if all the things that make us different are actually the things that make life extraordinary? Follow a colorful journey that encourages young readers to embrace individuality, kindness, and the joy of being exactly who they are.

Ordinary Smordinary celebrates acceptance and reminds readers that everyone deserves to feel seen, valued, and loved.

WHY THIS STORY MATTERS

There's no one right way to be wonderful.

For families, teachers, and young readers, this story opens the door to conversations about differences, self-esteem, and compassion. Because belonging should never require changing who you are.
