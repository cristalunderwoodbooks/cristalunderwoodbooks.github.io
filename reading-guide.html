READ · LISTEN · TALK

Conversation starters for curious hearts

These are invitations, not a test. Children can answer aloud, draw, write, gesture or just listen. There is no single right way to join in.

BEFORE READING

What makes you, you?

  • What is something you love doing?
  • Can friends enjoy different things and still be good friends?
  • What helps you feel comfortable and welcome?
WHILE READING

Notice the little moments

  • What feelings do you notice while reading or looking at the pictures?
  • Where could someone use a little kindness?
  • What would you ask a character about their day?
AFTER READING

What does kindness look like?

  • How can we include someone who does things differently?
  • How can we care for a friend without asking them to change?
  • What is one kind thing we can do this week?
FOR GROWN-UPS

Make room for every answer

  • Let children choose how they participate—or whether to pass.
  • Avoid asking someone to explain why they are different.
  • Welcome different ways of communicating, learning and playing.

A gentle reminder: It's okay to pause, take a movement break or try again another day. Feeling safe and understood matters more than finishing every question.
