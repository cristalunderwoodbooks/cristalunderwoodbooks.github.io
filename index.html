MEET THE STORYTELLER

Hi, I'm Cristal!

Writer. Narrator. Believer in the magic of being you.

I create stories filled with imagination, kindness, laughter, and heart. I want children to feel seen in the pages they read—and to know that the things that make them different can also make them wonderfully themselves.

From the colorful world of Ordinary Smordinary to the in-development Bailey Boo and the Stinky Shoe, my stories are invitations to celebrate what matters most: belonging, connection, and love.

When I'm not writing, you can often find me bringing someone else's story to life behind the microphone.
