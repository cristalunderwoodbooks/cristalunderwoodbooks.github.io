MEET THE STORYTELLER

Hi, I'm Cristal!

An author who believes every child deserves to feel understood.

Growing up in Erie, Pennsylvania, Cristal loved words, poetry, and the rhythm of a good story. But for much of her life, she felt different without fully understanding why.

At 57, an autism diagnosis helped Cristal make sense of experiences that had long felt confusing. As she continues to learn about herself, she is turning her journey toward self-acceptance into children's stories—including Ordinary Smordinary.

Her hope is simple: that every child knows they deserve kindness, friendship, and a place to belong.
