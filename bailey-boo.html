Bailey Boo &
the Stinky Shoe

One beloved dog. One very stinky shoe. A whole lot of love.

Meet Bailey Boo, a Golden Retriever whose favorite treasure isn't a fancy toy. It's an old, wonderfully stinky shoe filled with memories of the family he loves.

Inspired by a real and very special dog, this funny, tender story celebrates the adventures we share and the love that stays with us.
