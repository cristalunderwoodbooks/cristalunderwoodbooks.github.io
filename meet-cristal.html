THE STORY BEHIND THE STORIES

Meet Cristal

Sometimes understanding yourself changes the stories you want to tell.

Cristal Underwood is a children's author from Erie, Pennsylvania, who writes about kindness, individuality, and the many ways people experience the world. Her stories grow out of something personal: a lifelong journey toward understanding herself.

Explore Ordinary Smordinary →
01 · UNDERSTANDING HERSELF

Finding new understanding at 57.

✳

For much of her life, Cristal felt different without understanding why. Looking back, she has described trying to fit in and hiding parts of herself when she was younger.

At age 57, Cristal received an autism diagnosis. The diagnosis didn't change who she was; it helped her make sense of experiences that had felt confusing or isolating for years.

Her journey hasn't stopped there. Cristal is continuing to learn about herself, recognize her needs, and approach her own experiences with greater understanding. Her story is about learning to work with who she is—not trying to become someone else.

02 · A LOVE OF WORDS

The girl who never wanted a story to end.

✿

Cristal loved poetry, rhyming and stories long before she became a published author. Growing up in Erie, she received recognition for her writing and especially enjoyed the familiar rhythms of children's books.

There was one part of reading she never liked: arriving at the words “The End.” She wanted a favorite story to keep going.

That childhood feeling still inspires how she writes today. Her hope is that children will revisit her stories, finding something familiar, joyful and comforting in their pages.

03 · STORIES THAT MAKE ROOM

Turning hurtful labels into hopeful stories.

♡

Knowing what it feels like to be misunderstood has shaped the books Cristal wants to write. Words that once made her feel out of place are becoming invitations for children to talk about acceptance and kindness.

Her first children's book, Ordinary Smordinary, celebrates the beauty of being different. She has also shared plans for future stories with titles including Sensitive Smensitive, Difficult Smifficult, Anxious Smanxious and Nervous Smervous.

Cristal hopes parents, teachers and children can use her books to begin gentle conversations about differences, friendship and belonging.

THE HEART BEHIND THE BOOKS

You don't have to be like everyone else to belong.

That is the message Cristal hopes every young reader carries away: the things that make you different do not make you less worthy of respect, understanding or love.
